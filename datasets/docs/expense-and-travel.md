# Expense and Travel Policy

Owner: Finance · Last reviewed: 2026-03

## Approvals

- Under $500: your manager
- $500 to $5,000: a director
- Over $5,000: the CFO

Submit expenses within 45 days of the purchase.

## Per diem

The travel per diem covers meals and incidentals. It's $68 per day for travel within your home country and $85 per day for international travel. Employees who live in Duluth don't get a per diem when working at the Duluth office.

## Flights

Book economy for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.

## Home office and hardware

New employees get a $1,200 home office stipend when they join, then $400 each year after that.

## Learning budget

Each employee has $1,500 a year for courses, books and conference tickets. Travel to a conference comes out of the travel budget, not the learning budget.

## Not reimbursed

Alcohol isn't reimbursed, except at team events a director has approved in advance.
