# Time Off and Leave

Owner: People Ops · Last reviewed: 2026-02

## Paid time off

Tallowmoth has flexible PTO, and everyone is expected to take at least 15 days a year. For an absence of more than 3 consecutive working days, tell your manager at least 2 weeks ahead.

## Company holidays

Tallowmoth observes 10 public holidays, US or Canadian depending on where you live, plus Thaw Day, a company-wide holiday on the first Friday of March. The company also shuts down from December 24 through January 1.

## On-call during time off

If you're scheduled for on-call during planned time off, arrange a swap at least 5 business days before the shift starts and record it in Klaxon.

## Blackout periods

Customer Support doesn't approve PTO during the first two weeks of January, when customers run year-end compliance audits. Other teams have no blackout periods.

## Parental leave

All parents get 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken at any point within 12 months of a birth or adoption.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and has no fixed limit.
